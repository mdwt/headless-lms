title: Permissions

A permission is one resource and one action, such as `courses:read` or `communities:moderate`. Roles are made of permissions, and a connection or API key carries permissions too, as its scopes. It's one list.

## How a connection is checked

- **A key or app token carries permissions as scopes.** You choose them on the consent page, or when you create an API key, from the permissions you hold.
- **Each request is limited to your current permissions.** On every call, the connection holds its scopes and only those you hold at that moment. If your roles change, the connection changes with them on the next call. No connection can transfer ownership, even the owner's.
- **Each tool call is checked by the method it calls.** Every tool calls one service method, which checks the connection's permissions on the item it acts on and refuses the call otherwise.

A permission is about the organization or about one content type. A role given on the whole organization covers every item, and a role given on one course or community covers that item only. An Instructor of one course can call `update_course`, and the call succeeds only for that course.

## What is ticked for you

The consent page and the API key form start with every view permission ticked and every other permission cleared. Change permissions are opt-in. Delete permissions sit apart under a warning and are never ticked for you.

Some change and moderate permissions can also remove things: taking a file out of a download or an item out of a bundle, revoking someone's access, deleting a space or a space link, removing someone from a space, or taking down a post or a comment. The consent page says so next to each of them.

## Courses

- `courses:read`, `courses:write` and `courses:delete` are the course and everything in it: modules, activities and files uploaded for it.
- `courses:moderate` is the course's lesson comments: listing them, held ones included, approving, removing, restoring, replying and dismissing reports.
- `progress:read` is the course's learners, their progress and the course's analytics.

## Communities

- `communities:read`, `communities:write` and `communities:delete` are the structure: the community itself, its messaging settings, and its spaces with their topics and links. Deleting a space is a change to the community, under `communities:write`.
- `communities:moderate` is everything members post and who takes part: review queues, posts, comments, messages, flags, locked chats, space members and join requests.
- Granting access to a community is an access grant, under `access:write`, like a course or a download. Its roster of grant holders is `access:read`.
